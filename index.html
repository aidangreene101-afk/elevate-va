Privacy Policy.

Last updated July 2026

Elevate VA ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you submit the consultation form we collect the name, email address, chosen plan, and pain-point description you provide. When you use the client portal, any tasks you enter are stored locally in your own browser and are not transmitted to us unless you send them to us directly.

How we use it

We use your contact details only to respond to your enquiry, match you with a virtual assistant, and deliver the services you request. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

Form submissions are processed by our form provider, and payments (if you subscribe) are processed by Stripe. These providers handle your data under their own privacy policies. We use no advertising trackers on this site.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time by emailing us. To clear the tasks stored in your browser, use the "Clear all" control in the portal or clear your browser storage.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email our team.
