Privacy policy.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Elevate VA ("we," "us") is a virtual assistant service based in Old Lyme, Connecticut. This policy describes what information we collect through this website and how we handle it. The short version: we collect only what we need to respond to you and deliver our services, we don't sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

What we collect

When you submit our consultation form, we receive the information you enter: your name, email address, plan interest, and anything you tell us about your needs. Form submissions are processed by Formspree, which delivers them to our email. If you book a call, scheduling is handled by Cal.com, which collects the details you provide when booking. If you subscribe to a plan, payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your full card details — Stripe handles those under its own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliance.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to inquiries, provide the services you've engaged us for, send service-related communications, and process payments. If you become a client, information you share with us to perform work on your behalf is used solely for that work, treated as confidential, and never shared with third parties except as required to perform the services you've requested or as required by law.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising. This site does not use tracking cookies.

Data retention and your rights

We keep inquiry and client information for as long as needed to serve you, plus any period required for legal and accounting purposes. You may request a copy of your information or ask us to delete it by emailing us — we'll honor the request promptly unless we're legally required to retain something.
