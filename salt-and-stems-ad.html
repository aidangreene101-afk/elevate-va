Why This Converts

  • Hook leads with the experience, not the service. "Your guests become the florists" sells the moment; "build your own bouquet" only described the task.
  • Clear unique mechanism — a styled cart delivered to the venue — so it doesn't read as "just a florist."
  • Social proof (★★★★★ · 120+ celebrations) and scarcity (limited weekends, booking 2026) give a reason to act now.
  • One low-friction CTA: comment BLOOMS / Send Message starts a conversation instead of demanding a form.
  • High-contrast, mobile-first design — wine on cream reads on a phone at thumb-distance; the old creative was washed out.
  • Front-loaded primary text so the value lands before Meta's "…see more" truncation.

Replace ★ count, "120+" and "2026" with the client's real numbers before launch. Swap in a candid photo of an actual cart/event as the background for even stronger performance.
